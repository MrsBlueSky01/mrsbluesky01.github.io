## DevLog #3: Launching My Professional Portfolio with GitHub Pages and Jekyll

On the third day of my software journey, I officially launched my professional web presence. This stage was not just about content creation; it was a deep dive into the technical infrastructure and User Experience (UX) of building a modern digital identity.

### Infrastructure and Technology Stack

For hosting, I leveraged the **GitHub Pages** ecosystem, a favorite among modern web developers for its seamless integration. I chose **Jekyll** as my Static Site Generator (SSG). By utilizing this Ruby-based engine, I integrated high-speed performance and SEO advantages directly into my template, ensuring a lightweight and robust foundation.

### Visual Design and Dynamic Components

I adopted a "Modern Dark" aesthetic, customizing the palette with **deep pink accents** that reflect my personal brand. To elevate the design further, I implemented:

- **Neon Matrix Effect:** With the support of Gemini AI, I developed a background loop of flowing neon code snippets. This visual centerpiece provides a high-tech atmosphere that aligns with the site's technological core.
- **JS Portfolio Integration:** Instead of a standard static list, I transformed the portfolio section into an interactive experience. I engineered a **mini-game** using Vanilla JavaScript, allowing visitors to engage with my projects through an entertaining, gamified interface.

### Future Roadmap

This site is more than just a portfolio; it is a living documentation of my growth. Moving forward, I will be sharing my progress in both software and game development, as well as the technical evolution of this platform itself.

Stay coded, stay evolving.
